[h1]Immersive Solar Arrays [B42 MP][/h1]

[b]The classic solar power mod — completely overhauled, optimized, and modernized for Project Zomboid Build 42 (tested on 42.20) & Multiplayer![/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What's New in this B42 Version?[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Manual Backup Generator Connection:[/b] Say goodbye to unreliable automatic scanner bugs! You can now manually connect and disconnect your Backup Generator to your Battery Bank via the right-click context menu.
[*] [b]Full Build 42 Compatibility:[/b] Fully adapted to the B42 engine, including complete JSON translation dictionary support for [b]27 languages[/b] (200+ localized files).
[*] [b]Seamless Crafting & Build Menu Integration:[/b] All build recipes (Battery Bank, Solar Panels, Inverters, DIY Batteries, Failsafes) are cleanly integrated under the [b]Electrical[/b] category in the B42 Crafting / Build menu.
[*] [b]Tactical Box Dashboard UI:[/b] Redesigned native Lua Status Window for your Battery Bank — clean, performant, and perfectly fitting the PZ aesthetic.
[*] [b]Indoor Toxicity Protection:[/b] Battery Banks placed indoors will no longer emit lethal generator fumes ([i]"tötliche Dämpfe"[/i]) thanks to real-time B42 toxicity suppression.
[*] [b]Performance & Sound Optimizations:[/b] Drastically reduced garbage collection thrashing, intelligent spatial scan caching, and nil-safe audio support for Build 42.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How Does the Mod Work?[/h2]
[list=1]
[*] [b]Craft or Find Components:[/b] Open the Crafting Menu ([b]B key[/b]) under the [b]Electrical[/b] tab to craft Solar Panels, Inverters, Battery Banks, and accessories.
[*] [b]Deploy the Battery Bank:[/b] Place your Battery Bank on the ground or roof and insert car batteries or DIY batteries.
[*] [b]Connect Solar Panels:[/b] Place Solar Panels nearby and connect them to your Battery Bank via right-click.
[*] [b]Link a Backup Generator:[/b] Place a gas-powered generator within range and select [i]"Connect Backup Generator"[/i] from the right-click context menu. It will automatically start up whenever your batteries run low!
[/list]
